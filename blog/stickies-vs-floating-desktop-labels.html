Floating Desktop Labels vs. Apple Stickies: A 2026 Showdown

April 6, 2026 · 4 min read · Productivity

For years, Apple's built-in Stickies app has been the go-to for jotting down a quick thought on the Mac. It's simple, it's free, and it's familiar. But as our workflows have become more complex, revolving around multiple projects, virtual desktops (Spaces), and a constant need for focus, the limitations of this classic utility have become glaringly obvious.

Enter Floating Desktop Labels, a modern challenger designed for the power user. It's not just about notes; it's about context, organization, and maintaining flow. Is it time to upgrade from the trusty default? Let's break it down.

The Core Difference: Visibility

The single biggest frustration with Apple Stickies is that they get lost. The moment you open a new window, your important note is buried. You either have to expose your desktop or shuffle through windows to find it, breaking your concentration.

Floating Desktop Labels solves this with one key feature: its labels and notes can be set to float on top of all other windows. This means your to-do list, project name, or crucial reminder stays visible, no matter what you're working on.

Winner: Floating Desktop Labels, by a landslide. This is the feature you always wished Stickies had.

The Dealbreaker for Power Users: macOS Spaces

If you use macOS Spaces (virtual desktops) to separate your work, you've felt the pain. You switch to "Desktop 4" and waste precious seconds trying to remember if this is your 'Development' space or your 'Research' space.

Apple Stickies offers no solution here; a note lives on the desktop it was created on. Floating Desktop Labels was built specifically for this problem. You can create a label like "Project Phoenix" or "Client Comms" and place it in the corner of that Space. It acts as a permanent, always-visible identifier, allowing you to orient yourself instantly.

Aesthetics and Design

Stickies look... dated. They haven't had a significant design update in years. Floating Desktop Labels features 11 beautiful, semi-transparent "glass" themes that feel right at home on a modern macOS desktop. The ability to control opacity means you can make your notes as subtle or as prominent as you need.

The Question of Price

Stickies is free. Floating Desktop Labels is a one-time purchase.

Think of it as the difference between a free, basic tool and a professional-grade instrument. Stickies is fine for a grocery list. But if you're a developer, a project manager, a student, or any professional who juggles multiple contexts, the productivity gain from having persistent, space-aware, always-visible notes isn't just a convenience—it's a workflow enhancement. The one-time cost is an investment in focus.

The Verdict

Apple Stickies is for the casual user who needs to occasionally write down a phone number. It's a digital post-it note in its most basic form.

Floating Desktop Labels is for the professional. It's a productivity tool designed for the way people actually work on Macs today. It brings order to the chaos of virtual desktops and ensures your most important information is never lost in a sea of windows.

If you're still using Stickies and constantly find yourself losing notes behind windows or getting lost in your Spaces, the upgrade is a no-brainer.
